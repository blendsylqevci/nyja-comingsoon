29NYJA · Coming Soon · V1.1 — THE FIRST CONNECTION, geographic · three countries, three capitals, one connection · boards A–J · refinement of V1 (turn 28)
What changed
The opening network is now geography: Kosovo, Albania and North Macedonia drawn from Natural Earth 1:10m data in a Mercator projection, with Prishtinë, Tiranë and Shkup at their GeoNames coordinates. The three capitals connect; scrolling turns those three connections into the Y of the logo — a continuous point-for-point morph (geography → pinched network → knot) that ends on the official NYJA ICON — the knot alone, no tile, rendered from the approved vector (uploads/NYJA_Logo_Icon-12.svg), revealed outward from the connections, no crossfade; knot in the same neon #D5FF00 as the logo on the page. Statement, form, footer, CTA, motion modes and accessibility states are V1.
Behaviour
Intro (≈27s, skippable): borders draw → capitals → the NYJA van leaves Prishtinë and tours 23 cities in 24s (Kosovo → Albania → North Macedonia), each name lighting as it arrives, its trail staying on the map as the network → the three capital connections → the cue. Returning visitors get the 600ms short entry with the trail already laid. The stage is pinned on desktop and mobile; the first 600px of scroll (400 on mobile) drive the transformation: land and labels fade, the triangle pinches into one junction, the knot forms, the asset fades over the schematic; at the end the period of LIDHET. takes the material and one connection runs to the wordmark. Hovering a capital lifts its country. 29a and 29h1 are live. Tweaks: motion, visit, formState.
Verification
Geography: 29j (source, projection, coordinates).
